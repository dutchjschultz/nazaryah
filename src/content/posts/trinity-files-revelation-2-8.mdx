**Revelation 2:8**

**The First and the Last**

*Smyrna's title — who died, and is alive*

— The Standing Stone —

> **Behind "LORD" in your Bible lies a hidden name — in the Hebrew it is Yahuah** *Psalm 83:18*; **Yahuah is the Father** *Isaiah 63:16*; **Yahuah is the only God, beside Him there is no other** *Isaiah 45:5*; **therefore Yahuah the Father is the only true God, leaving no room for a second or third person** *1 Corinthians 8:6*.

Reference Piece

**The Titles He Was Given**

*A title of Yahuah on the Son is worn by gift — a shared title was never a shared identity*

[Foundation: The Titles He Was Given →](/foundations/the-titles-he-was-given)

***1 — The Claim***

To the assembly at Smyrna, Yahushua announces himself again as “the first and the last” — Yahuah's own title from Isaiah — so again the title is read as a claim to be Yahuah.

***2 — The Title Comes With Its Own Definition***

As at the opening of the book, the title is not left to stand alone. The same clause that carries it tells you who the speaker is:

> **Revelation 2:8**
>
> *These things saith the first and the last, which was dead, and is alive…*

“Which was dead, and is alive.” The One who holds the title died and was raised — which the eternal Yahuah, of whom death was never true, did not and could not do. The words are Isaiah's; the meaning is set by the grave and the empty tomb. He is first and last as the firstborn of the new creation, not as a second timeless God beside the One who has no beginning.

Conclusion

**The Verdict**

> **“The first and the last, which was dead, and is alive.” The One who died is not the deathless God — he is the first to rise, and never die again.**
