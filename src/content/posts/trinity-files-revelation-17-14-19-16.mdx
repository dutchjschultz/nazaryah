**Revelation 17:14; 19:16**

**A Name Written On Him**

*King of kings — crowned, not self-made*

— The Standing Stone —

> **Behind "LORD" in your Bible lies a hidden name — in the Hebrew it is Yahuah** *Psalm 83:18*; **Yahuah is the Father** *Isaiah 63:16*; **Yahuah is the only God, beside Him there is no other** *Isaiah 45:5*; **therefore Yahuah the Father is the only true God, leaving no room for a second or third person** *1 Corinthians 8:6*.

Reference Piece

**The Titles He Was Given**

*A title of Yahuah on the Son is worn by gift — a shared title was never a shared identity*

[Foundation: The Titles He Was Given →](/foundations/the-titles-he-was-given)

***1 — The Claim***

Yahushua is called “King of kings, and Lord of lords” — a title Scripture gives to Yahuah *1 Timothy 6:15* — so to wear it, the argument runs, is to be God.

***2 — Written On Him, and Won as the Lamb***

First, the title is not a divine-only name: Scripture lays “king of kings” on Nebuchadnezzar, and says plainly it was given him by God *Daniel 2:37* — the point the foundation carries in full. Then see how Revelation places it on Yahushua:

> **Revelation 19:16**
>
> *And he hath on his vesture and on his thigh a name written, KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS.*

A name written on a man is a name given to a man. And he wins the title as the Lamb — by overcoming, not by native right:

> **Revelation 17:14**
>
> *These shall make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them: for he is Lord of lords, and King of kings…*

Even where the full title belongs to God, the two stay distinct: it is “the blessed and only Potentate, the King of kings” who shows the appearing of “our Lord Jesus Christ” *1 Timothy 6:15* — title-holder and the one he reveals, named as two in one sentence.

Conclusion

**The Verdict**

> **A name written on a man is a name given to a man. Yahushua is King of kings because Yahuah crowned him — not because he is Yahuah.**
