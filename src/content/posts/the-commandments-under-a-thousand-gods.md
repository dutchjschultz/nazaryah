## The Oldest Counterfeit

Hinduism is the oldest of the great religions still practiced. Its earliest writings, the Vedas, go back more than a thousand years before Messiah. It has no single founder and no single creed. It holds millions of gods, its own triad of gods, gods who come down in human form, and a temple at nearly every crossroads in India.

Still, like Buddhism, which grew out of it, Hinduism kept much of the second tablet of the Law. Its fault is not with the neighbour. Its fault is with Yahuah.

## What Hinduism Kept

**The second tablet.** Hindu teaching lists five restraints every seeker should keep: do no harm, speak the truth, do not steal, keep sexual purity, and do not grasp after what is not yours. Set them beside the Law: "Thou shalt not kill," "Thou shalt not bear false witness," "Thou shalt not steal," "Thou shalt not commit adultery," and "Thou shalt not covet" (Exodus 20:13-17). That is commandments six through ten. An old Hindu saying tells a child to treat his mother and his father as a god, and the fifth commandment says "Honour thy father and thy mother" (Exodus 20:12).

**Set prayer.** The devout Hindu prays at the three joinings of the day: dawn, noon, and dusk. David prayed "evening, and morning, and at noon" (Psalm 55:17), and Daniel three times a day (Daniel 6:10).

**Fasting and diet.** Hindus fast often, many of them twice each lunar month. Millions keep a diet free of meat and blood. Daniel and his friends asked for "pulse to eat, and water to drink" rather than the king's meat (Daniel 1:12).

**A calendar of sun and moon.** The Hindu calendar counts its months by the moon and adds a month when needed to keep the year in its season. Islam threw the sun away. The church threw the moon away. Hinduism kept both, then filled the calendar with feasts for other gods.

Paul explained it: the Gentiles "do by nature the things contained in the law," and their conscience bears witness to "the work of the law written in their hearts" (Romans 2:14-15).

## What Hinduism Broke

**The first commandment.** "Thou shalt have no other gods before me" (Exodus 20:3). Hinduism has more gods than any people on earth. Moses told Israel, "Yahuah our God is one Yahuah" (Deuteronomy 6:4), and Yahuah said, "I am Yahuah, and there is none else, there is no God beside me" (Isaiah 45:5).

**The second commandment.** Hindu worship centers on images of stone, metal, and wood, washed, dressed, fed, and carried in procession. The prophets mocked this exact practice. Idols "have mouths, but they speak not: eyes have they, but they see not," and "they that make them are like unto them" (Psalm 115:5-8). Isaiah described a man who burns half a tree to cook his food and bows down to the other half (Isaiah 44:15-17). Paul said men "worshipped and served the creature more than the Creator" (Romans 1:25). In Hinduism the cow, the river, and the snake all receive that service.

**A triad of gods.** Hinduism teaches the Trimurti, three gods in one divine work: Brahma who creates, Vishnu who preserves, and Shiva who destroys. Pagan nations had their triads long before any church council voted a three-in-one God into its creed. A threefold god is the pattern of the nations, not of Israel. Israel's confession had one word for the count of God, and the word was one.

**Gods in human flesh.** Hindu teaching says the god Vishnu has come down to earth again and again in human and animal forms, as Rama and as Krishna. Scripture says the opposite: "God is not a man" (Numbers 23:19). Yahuah did not come down as His own Son. He sent His Son, a man born of a woman (Galatians 4:4), "the man Messiah Yahushua" (1 Timothy 2:5).

**Rebirth and caste.** Hinduism teaches that a soul is born again and again, rising or falling by its deeds, and it once sorted men into castes from birth by that rule. Scripture answers both. "It is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment" (Hebrews 9:27). And "God is no respecter of persons" (Acts 10:34).

## The Lesson for the Church

The Hindu prays at dawn, noon, and dusk, fasts twice a month, and will not steal or lie. He does all of it before an idol. The church claims the one true God and does almost none of it. Hinduism shows what the second tablet looks like when the first is torn out. It becomes discipline in the service of other gods. The church shows the reverse: a God claimed with the discipline thrown away.

## Conclusion

Hinduism kept the neighbour and lost the Maker. Its prayers are set at the right hours and aimed at the wrong gods. Its fasting is real, its honesty is real, and its idols are still stone. Every good thing it kept came from the Law written on the heart. Every ruin in it came from breaking the first two commandments.

Revelation calls the nations out of exactly this. The angel cries with a loud voice, "Fear God, and give glory to him," and "worship him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters" (Revelation 14:7). That is the Creator, not the creature, and one God, not a thousand.
