The six-pointed star carries David's name, but David never saw it. Not one verse of Scripture commands it, describes it, or mentions it. The people who were given a commanded emblem, the lampstand of pure gold (Exodus 25:31), set it aside in modern times for a shape the nations also used.

## The Shield Was Never a Shape

David's shield was not an emblem. It was Yahuah. "Thou, O Yahuah, art a shield for me" (Psalm 3:3). "He is a buckler to all those that trust in him" (Psalm 18:30). Turning that confession into a geometric figure moves trust from the One who protects to a figure that represents protection. It is the same step Israel took with the bronze serpent, which Hezekiah had to break (2 Kings 18:4).

## The Star of Your God

Scripture does speak of a star carried by Israel, and it speaks against it. Amos told the house of Israel that they had borne "the star of your god, which ye made to yourselves" (Amos 5:26), and Stephen repeated it as "the star of your god Remphan" (Acts 7:43). Many teachers claim that star was the hexagram. The historical record does not show what shape it had, so that claim cannot be proven and is not pressed here. What can be said plainly is this: the only time Scripture mentions Israel carrying a star as a religious sign, it is a rebuke for idolatry.

## The Star That Was Promised

Scripture does foretell a star, and it is not a shape. Balaam prophesied, "there shall come a Star out of Jacob, and a Sceptre shall rise out of Israel" (Numbers 24:17). That Star is a man, a king from Jacob's line. The wise men came seeking Him: "we have seen his star in the east, and are come to worship him" (Matthew 2:2).

The promised Star arrived. Judaism, rejecting Yahushua, kept the hope of a Star and filled the empty place with a six-pointed figure. The symbol stands where the Messiah should be.

## The Verdict

The Star of David is a medieval community mark raised to a national flag. It gives David's name to a shape he never knew, turns the shield that was Yahuah Himself into a figure, and holds the place of the Star that Scripture promised and Yahuah sent.

Revelation names that Star. "I am the root and the offspring of David, and the bright and morning star" (Revelation 22:16). The true star of David is the Son of David.
