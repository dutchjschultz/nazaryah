## The Question

Where did the tree of life go after Eden?

Most readers of Genesis assume it was simply lost. Man sinned, he was driven out, and the tree disappeared from the story until it shows up again in the last chapter of Revelation. Between those two pages, it seems, there is nothing.

Scripture tells it differently. The tree was not destroyed. It was guarded (Genesis 3:24). And over and over, through the rest of the Old Testament, Yahuah built a picture of it again, in His house, in the hands of His priest, and in the words of His prophet.

## What Scripture Does Not Say

Scripture never names the kind of tree that stood in the midst of the garden. Genesis names the fig, whose leaves Adam and Eve sewed together (Genesis 3:7). It does not name the tree of life. No one can open the Bible and read, “the tree of life was an almond tree.”

This investigation does not claim that. It makes a different claim, and one the text can carry.

## The Claim

Every time Yahuah built the tree of life again, He built it as an almond. And every time, He set it beside His Law.

The only tree Yahuah ever designed with His own instructions was an almond tree of gold, and He placed it in a house laid out like Eden, before the testimony (Exodus 25:31-34; 27:21). The rod He brought to life out of dead wood was an almond rod, and He kept it with the tables of the covenant (Numbers 17:8, 10; Hebrews 9:4). The place where Jacob saw the gate of heaven was first called Luz, which means almond (Genesis 28:17-19).

Why the almond? Yahuah answered that Himself. The almond in Hebrew is shaqed, the watching tree, and He told Jeremiah, “I will hasten my word to perform it,” using the word shoqed, watching (Jeremiah 1:11-12). The almond is the tree that keeps watch over Yahuah’s word. That is why it always stands next to the Law.

## The Five Witnesses

Witness One — [**The Tree Without a Name**](/blog/the-tree-without-a-name) (Genesis 2:9; 3:22-24). Eden’s tree is placed in the midst, then guarded at the east. It is kept, not destroyed, and its kind is never given.

Witness Two — [**The Gate at Almond**](/blog/the-gate-at-almond) (Genesis 28:10-22; 35:1-15). Jacob sees the gate of heaven at Luz, the almond place, and Yahuah repeats His promise there.

Witness Three — [**Rebuilt in Gold**](/blog/rebuilt-in-gold) (Exodus 25:31-40; 27:20-21). Yahuah gives Moses the pattern of an almond tree of gold, set inside an Eden-shaped house, before the testimony.

Witness Four — [**Dead Wood Lives**](/blog/dead-wood-lives) (Numbers 17; Joshua 8:33). An almond rod buds, blossoms, and bears fruit overnight, and is kept with the tables of the Law.

Witness Five — [**The Watching Tree**](/blog/the-watching-tree) (Jeremiah 1:11-12; 31:28). The almond’s own name explains the pattern: Yahuah watches over His word to perform it.

## Right There All Along

Many believers today have been taught to set the Law against life, as if the commandments were the dead branch and freedom from them were the living one. Scripture keeps putting them side by side. The golden almond tree faced the testimony. The living almond rod lay beside the tables of the covenant. And Moses, standing near the same ark, told Israel what the choice was: “I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life” (Deuteronomy 30:19).

Proverbs says the same thing in plain words. It opens with “forget not my law; but let thine heart keep my commandments,” and then says of wisdom, “She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her” (Proverbs 3:1, 18).

None of this was hidden. It has been in the text the whole time, waiting for someone to notice the tree standing next to the tablets.

## Where the Tree Stands at the End

In Eden, the way to the tree was shut. In the house of Yahuah, a tree of almonds stood inside, watching. In the last pages of Scripture, the city needs no sun, “for the glory of Elohim did lighten it, and the Lamb is the light thereof” (Revelation 21:23). The word for light there is luchnos, a lamp. The glory is the light, and the Lamb is the lamp that carries it, just as the almond tree of gold carried the light in the Holy Place.

And the tree is open once more, to the very people the Law described: “Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life” (Revelation 22:14).
