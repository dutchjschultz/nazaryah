## What Came Into the Church

The Enneagram is a personality system drawn as a circle with nine points joined by lines. Each point is a "type" — the Reformer, the Helper, the Achiever, and so on — and each type is said to have a hidden core sin or fear. Over the past thirty years it has become popular in Catholic retreats and then in evangelical churches, small groups, and Christian bookstores. A 2016 evangelical book, *The Road Back to You*, carried it into the mainstream.

## The Claimed Origin

The Enneagram is often said to be an ancient Sufi teaching, passed down in secret by Islamic mystics. The record does not support that. No Sufi text containing the system has been produced.

What the record does show is this. The nine-pointed symbol was taught in the 1910s and 1920s by G. I. Gurdjieff, an occult teacher of Greek and Armenian descent from the Russian Empire who said he learned it from a hidden brotherhood in Central Asia; no such brotherhood has ever been documented. In the 1960s Oscar Ichazo, a Bolivian spiritual teacher, attached nine personality "fixations" to the symbol and taught it at his Arica school in Chile. In the early 1970s Claudio Naranjo, a Chilean psychiatrist, brought it to the Esalen Institute in California, a center of the New Age movement. From there Jesuit priests carried it into Catholic seminaries and retreat houses, and from Catholic circles it passed into Protestant churches.

So the Enneagram is not Christian, and it is not proven to be Sufi. It is an occult symbol given a personality system by New Age teachers, then baptized by the church.

## What Scripture Says

The Enneagram promises to reveal the hidden heart. Scripture says only one can do that: "The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked: who can know it? I Yahuah search the heart, I try the reins" (Jeremiah 17:9-10). David did not ask a chart to read him; he asked Yahuah: "Search me, O God, and know my heart" (Psalm 139:23).

Nor does Scripture sort sinners into nine types with nine paths to growth. It has one standard for every man, the Law, and one way of deliverance, the blood of the Lamb. A system that tells a man his sin is part of his "type" gives him a reason to keep it.

## The Verdict

The Enneagram is strange fire in the sanctuary — an occult symbol with a New Age personality chart, carried into the church by priests and sold to believers as self-knowledge. The heart is known by its Maker and measured by His Law, not by a nine-pointed circle.

Revelation gives the Son's own claim to that work: "I am he which searcheth the reins and hearts: and I will give unto every one of you according to your works" (Revelation 2:23).
