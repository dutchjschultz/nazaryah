The heart step of the walk begins with one sentence. Moses spoke it to the generation at the border, and Yahushua named it the first of all the commandments.

## Hear

"Hear, O Israel: Yahuah our Elohim is one Yahuah" (6:4). The first word, *shema*, means more than to let sound reach the ear. It means to listen and act on what is heard. Israel's whole walk begins with hearing who Yahuah is.

## One

The word for one is *echad*. Some teachers say it means a compound unity, a oneness made of several persons. Scripture uses the same word for a single man: "Abraham was one" (Ezekiel 33:24). When a scribe asked Yahushua which commandment was first, He quoted the Shema (Mark 12:29–30). The scribe answered, "there is one Elohim; and there is none other but he," and Yahushua said, "Thou art not far from the kingdom" (Mark 12:32–34). Paul writes it the same way: "to us there is but one Elohim, the Father" (1 Corinthians 8:6).

## With All the Heart

The command follows at once: "thou shalt love Yahuah thy Elohim with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy might" (6:5). One Elohim calls for one undivided heart. A heart split between Yahuah and the world's gods is the very thing the Shema rules out.

## On the Heart, In the House, On the Road

"These words… shall be in thine heart" (6:6). From there they spread outward. They are taught to the children "when thou sittest in thine house, and when thou walkest by the way, and when thou liest down, and when thou risest up" (6:7). The Shema shapes the whole day and the whole walk, not one hour of the week.

## Hand, Eyes, and Door

The words are bound "for a sign upon thine hand," set "as frontlets between thine eyes," and written "upon the posts of thy house, and on thy gates" (6:8–9). The hand is what a man does, the eyes are what he looks at and thinks on, and the door is his household. Yahuah's words were to mark all three.

## Revelation

Revelation shows two marks on the same two places. The beast causes all to receive a mark "in their right hand, or in their foreheads" (Revelation 13:16). Those with the Lamb have "his Father's name written in their foreheads" (Revelation 14:1). The hand and the forehead the Shema claimed for Yahuah's words are claimed by one master or the other at the end.
