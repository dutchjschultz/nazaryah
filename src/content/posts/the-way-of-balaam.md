## The Prophet for Hire

Balaam son of Beor lived at Pethor by the river, far to the east (Numbers 22:5). He was a known diviner, and Joshua later calls him "the soothsayer" (Joshua 13:22). Balak king of Moab, afraid of Israel camped on his border, sent elders to him "with the rewards of divination in their hand" (Numbers 22:7). Balaam knew Yahuah's answer from the first night, "thou shalt not curse the people: for they are blessed" (Numbers 22:12), but he kept the door open for the money.

## The Donkey

On the road, the angel of Yahuah stood in the way with a drawn sword. The donkey saw him and Balaam did not. Three times the animal turned aside, three times Balaam beat it, and then Yahuah opened its mouth (Numbers 22:28). Peter's summary is blunt: "the dumb ass speaking with man's voice forbad the madness of the prophet" (2 Peter 2:16). The animal saw more clearly than the man who sold his sight.

## Blessings He Could Not Stop

Balak built altar after altar, and every time Balaam opened his mouth a blessing came out. "How shall I curse, whom El hath not cursed?" (Numbers 23:8). "Elohim is not a man, that he should lie" (Numbers 23:19). Yahuah "hath not beheld iniquity in Jacob" (Numbers 23:21). A later oracle looks far ahead: "there shall come a Star out of Jacob, and a Sceptre shall rise out of Israel" (Numbers 24:17). Moses later put it plainly: Yahuah "turned the curse into a blessing unto thee, because Yahuah thy Elohim loved thee" (Deuteronomy 23:5).

## What the Curse Could Not Do, the Counsel Did

The next chapter opens with Israel in the tents of Moab's daughters, eating at their sacrifices and bowing to their gods. Israel "joined himself unto Baal-peor" (Numbers 25:3), and twenty-four thousand died in the plague (Numbers 25:9). Only later does the text name the source: these women caused Israel to sin "through the counsel of Balaam" (Numbers 31:16). He could not curse Israel from the outside, so he showed Balak how to draw Israel into sin from the inside. Balaam himself died by the sword among the Midianites (Numbers 31:8).

## Three Apostolic Warnings

- **Peter:** false teachers follow "the way of Balaam," who "loved the wages of unrighteousness" (2 Peter 2:15).

- **Jude:** they "ran greedily after the error of Balaam for reward" (Jude 11).

- **Yahushua:** some at Pergamos held "the doctrine of Balaam," who taught Balak to make Israel eat things sacrificed to idols and commit fornication (Revelation 2:14).

## The Way of Balaam Today

The pattern has not changed. Preaching for profit sells the word of Yahuah at a price, just as Balaam did. And the enemy still does not need to curse those Yahuah has blessed. He invites them to the table instead, mixing the worship of Yahuah with the feasts and customs of other gods until His people join themselves to Baal-peor while still calling on His name.

## Revelation

To those at Pergamos who held Balaam's doctrine, Yahushua gave one command: repent, "or else I will come unto thee quickly, and will fight against them with the sword of my mouth" (Revelation 2:16). Balaam fell by the sword in Numbers, and his doctrine falls by the sword in Revelation.
