## The Charge Answered First

Anyone who points out where Islam got something right will soon hear the charge: "You think like a Muslim." The charge has it backward. Every practice in this study was written in Scripture long before Islam existed. Moses gave the Shema some fifteen centuries before Messiah was born. Daniel prayed three times a day in Babylon more than eleven centuries before Muhammad. The apostles confessed one God and kept a clean table six hundred years before Islam rose in Arabia in the 600s AD.

Islam took Abraham, Moses, the prophets, and a good part of the Law out of the Bible and carried them into its own book. When a Muslim keeps something written in Torah, he is keeping a page the church tore out. The question is not why the Muslim looks like the Bible in places. The question is why the church does not.

Scripture itself uses this kind of comparison to shame Yahuah's own people. Through Jeremiah, Yahuah said that "backsliding Israel hath justified herself more than treacherous Judah" (Jeremiah 3:11). Yahushua said that "the men of Nineveh shall rise in judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it" (Matthew 12:41). Outsiders who obeyed a little were set beside insiders who knew everything and obeyed nothing. That is the comparison made here.

The Muslim is right about several things and wrong about the one thing that matters most. Both are set down plainly below.

## One God

When Yahushua was asked which commandment came first, He answered with the Shema: "Hear, O Israel: Yahuah our God is one Yahuah" (Mark 12:29; Deuteronomy 6:4). He called His Father "the only true God" and named Himself separately as the one "whom thou hast sent" (John 17:3). Paul said the same: "to us there is but one God, the Father" (1 Corinthians 8:6), and "one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Messiah Yahushua" (1 Timothy 2:5). The Son confessed the one God. He never counted Himself into the count.

Islam holds to one God. The confession that God is one stands at the center of the Quran, and it tells its readers plainly not to say "three" (Surah 4:171). A Muslim child can see what a seminary graduate has been trained not to see.

The early assemblies held this too, until the councils voted the Trinity into the creeds at Nicaea in 325 AD and Constantinople in 381 AD, three centuries after the apostles died. The Muslim kept the first commandment the way Yahushua quoted it. The church rewrote it.

## Set Times of Prayer

David said, "Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I pray, and cry aloud: and he shall hear my voice" (Psalm 55:17). Daniel kneeled "three times a day" with his windows open toward Jerusalem, with the lions' den waiting for him (Daniel 6:10). The apostles kept the hours after the resurrection. Peter and John went up to the temple "at the hour of prayer, being the ninth hour" (Acts 3:1). Peter went up on the housetop to pray "about the sixth hour" (Acts 10:9). Cornelius prayed at the ninth hour in his house (Acts 10:30).

Muslims pray at five set times every day: before dawn, at midday, in the afternoon, at dusk, and at night. The Quran itself names prayer at the two ends of the day and in the night (Surah 11:114; 17:78). The fixed count of five comes from the later hadith traditions. Islam stretched the three into five, but even stretched, it kept the habit the church dropped.

In its first years Islam prayed facing Jerusalem, as Daniel did. Around 624 AD the direction was turned to Mecca (Surah 2:142-144). That one turn sums up the whole religion. It began facing the right city and turned its back on it.

Two more prayer habits come straight from Scripture. Muslims wash their hands, face, and feet before prayer. The priests of Israel washed their hands and feet before they ministered, "that they die not" (Exodus 30:19-21), and believers are made "kings and priests unto God" (Revelation 1:6; 1 Peter 2:9). Muslims also fall face down to the ground, as Yahushua Himself "fell on his face, and prayed" in the garden (Matthew 26:39).

The church prays before meals, for an hour on Sunday, and when trouble comes. Set times of prayer are waved off as legalism, yet the apostles of Messiah kept them.

## The Clean Table

Torah names the swine unclean: "of their flesh shall ye not eat, and their carcase shall ye not touch" (Leviticus 11:7-8; Deuteronomy 14:8). Isaiah carries the rule to the end of the age. When Yahuah comes "with fire," those "eating swine's flesh, and the abomination, and the mouse, shall be consumed together" (Isaiah 66:15-17). That is a prophecy about His coming, not a rule for an age long past.

Islam forbids pork, blood, the dead carcass, and meat offered to other gods (Surah 2:173; 5:3). Set that beside what the apostles laid on Gentile believers at Jerusalem: "abstain from meats offered to idols, and from blood, and from things strangled, and from fornication" (Acts 15:29). A strangled animal is one whose blood was never drained. The Muslim table follows the Jerusalem council's list more closely than the church table does.

Stated correctly, halal is not Leviticus 11. Muslims eat camel, which Torah names unclean by name (Leviticus 11:4), and most Muslim schools allow shellfish, which has neither fins nor scales (Leviticus 11:10-12). Islam kept the pork, the blood, and the idols, and missed the rest.

## A Fixed Day

Here the Muslim deserves the least credit. Muslims gather on Friday for a midday congregational prayer (Surah 62:9). It is not a Sabbath. The very next verse tells them, once the prayer is done, to scatter through the land and seek profit (Surah 62:10). An hour of worship, then back to the market. Friday is the sixth day of the Roman week, and the Muslim calendar day begins at dusk, the Babylonian reckoning.

What the Muslim keeps is a fixed weekly assembly that the whole community holds and will not shift for convenience. The church gathers on the first day, set by Constantine's Sunday law of 321 AD and by the Council of Laodicea around 364 AD, which forbade believers to rest on the Sabbath. Neither one keeps Yahuah's Sabbath.

Yahuah set His calendar in the lights of heaven (Genesis 1:14; Psalm 104:19). The month renews with the moon, and the Sabbaths fall on the eighth, fifteenth, twenty-second, and twenty-ninth of every month.

Islam kept the moon and threw away the sun. Its months begin with the moon, but its year runs about 354 days and is never brought back into season. The Quran forbids holding a month back to correct it (Surah 9:36-37). Because of this, Ramadan wanders through every season of the year, once around about every thirty-three years. Torah ties the first month to the land: "Observe the month of Abib" (Deuteronomy 16:1), the month of the green ear. The church went the other way. It kept the Roman sun calendar and threw away the moon. Yahuah's calendar needs both: the sun turns the year, and the moon numbers the month.

## The Yearly Fast

Torah appoints a fast on Yom Kippur: "ye shall afflict your souls" (Leviticus 23:27). Yahushua said "when ye fast," not if (Matthew 6:16-17).

Muslims fast a full month every year, with no food or water, from the first light of dawn until night (Surah 2:187). The fast follows the light of the day, dawn to dusk. Stated correctly, Ramadan is not a feast of Yahuah, Yom Kippur is the appointed fast, and Ramadan drifts out of season. The credit here is for the discipline. A billion people go without bread every year because they believe they were told to. Most churchgoers have never missed a meal for Yahuah in their lives.

## Where Islam Went Sideways

### It denies the Son

The Quran says God is far above having a son (Surah 4:171). John answers that directly: "He is antichrist, that denieth the Father and the Son. Whosoever denieth the Son, the same hath not the Father" (1 John 2:22-23). The Muslim has the right number. He has one God. But he refuses the Son that one God sent (John 17:3), and the one who refuses the Son does not have the Father either.

### It denies the cross

Islam honors Isa as the Messiah and a prophet, then takes away the one thing He came to do. The Quran says he was neither killed nor crucified, and that it was only made to appear so (Surah 4:157). With no death there is no blood. Torah says "the life of the flesh is in the blood," and "it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul" (Leviticus 17:11). Yahuah provided the Lamb (Genesis 22:8), and the Lamb was slain at Pesach. Islam has no Pesach.

Deliverance comes in two stages. Entry is through the blood: "being now justified by his blood, we shall be saved from wrath through him" (Romans 5:9). The walk that follows is by the bread, the commandments. Islam has prayer, fasting, a clean table, and giving to the poor. It has pieces of the bread with no blood. Every work in Islam is a man trying to walk a road he never entered by the door. Yahuah saves, and the Son provided the way. Islam rejects the way.

### It gave the Prophet's mantle to the wrong man

Moses promised a Prophet "from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me" (Deuteronomy 18:15). Muslims claim that Prophet is Muhammad and read "brethren" as the sons of Ishmael. But "from the midst of thee" is Israel. Peter names Yahushua as that Prophet (Acts 3:22-26), and so does Stephen (Acts 7:37). Yahuah said of that Prophet, "I will put my words in his mouth" (Deuteronomy 18:18), and Yahushua said, "the word which ye hear is not mine, but the Father's which sent me" (John 14:24).

### It lost the Name

Allah is the Arabic word for God. It is a title, not a name, and Arab-speaking Christians use the same word. Yahuah's Name appears nowhere in the Quran. Yahuah said of His Name, "this is my name for ever, and this is my memorial unto all generations" (Exodus 3:15). A religion that confesses one God and cannot say His Name has kept the count and lost the One counted.

### It replaced the Book

Islam teaches that the Torah and the Gospel were corrupted and that the Quran stands over them. Scripture gives the test for any later book: "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them" (Isaiah 8:20). The Quran fails that test at the Son, at the cross, and at the Name.

## What the Church Must Answer

The church has the Son's name and the cross. It also threw away nearly everything Islam kept. It traded the one God for a Trinity. It traded set prayer for a Sunday hour. It traded the clean table for the pork barbecue. It traded Yahuah's Sabbath for Constantine's first day. It traded the fast for a feast. It did all of this so the road would be easier, and Yahushua warned that the easy road is the broad one: "wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction" (Matthew 7:13).

Yahushua said, "If ye love me, keep my commandments" (John 14:15).

## Conclusion

Islam kept pieces of the Law without the Lamb, and pieces of the Law without the Lamb cannot save anyone. The church kept the Lamb's name and threw away the Law, and a name without obedience is not belief. One has the walk with no door. The other has the door and refuses the walk. The way Yahuah gave is both: entry through the blood of the Son He provided, then a walk by the bread of His commandments.

So the Muslim does stand in judgment over the church, the same way Nineveh stood over Israel. A people without the Son kept more of the Father's Law than a people who claim the Son. That should shame the church into repentance, not into argument.

Revelation names the people who stand at the end: "Here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Yahushua" (Revelation 14:12). Islam has some of the commandments and refuses the testimony. The church claims the testimony and refuses the commandments. Only those who hold both are counted among the saints when He comes.
