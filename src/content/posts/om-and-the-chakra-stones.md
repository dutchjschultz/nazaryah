## The Om Pendant

The Om symbol — a curved figure like the number 3 with a tail, a crescent, and a dot — appears on necklaces, tattoos, yoga mats, and studio walls across America. In Hinduism, Om is the most sacred sound. The Upanishads, the Hindu scriptures, teach that Om is the sound of Brahman, the god-force behind all things, and that all of creation is contained in it. It is chanted at the start and end of prayers and mantras. A person wearing an Om pendant is wearing the central name of Hindu worship.

## The Chakra Stones

The colored stones sold as "healing crystals" or "chakra stones" are usually sold in sets of seven, one for each color of the rainbow: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet. The claim is that each stone gives off an energy that opens or balances one of seven "chakras," the energy centers said to run up the body from the base of the spine to the crown of the head. Amethyst is sold for the crown, lapis for the brow, rose quartz for the heart, and so on.

The chakras are Hindu. They come from tantric texts written in India from about the ninth and tenth centuries on, and those texts list differing numbers of chakras. The rainbow set sold in America is not from India at all. Historian Kurt Leland traced it in his book *Rainbow Body* (2016): Western Theosophists in the late 1800s and early 1900s fixed the count at seven and began assigning colors; writers of the 1970s laid the rainbow over them; and the version now sold everywhere came together in 1977. Crystal healing was added by the New Age movement of the 1980s. The chakra stones are a Western invention built on a Hindu idea.

## Yahuah's Stones

Scripture has its own set of stones, and they were never charms. The high priest wore a breastplate set with twelve precious stones, and "the stones shall be with the names of the children of Israel, twelve" (Exodus 28:21). Aaron was to "bear the names of the children of Israel ... upon his heart, when he goeth in unto the holy place, for a memorial before Yahuah continually" (Exodus 28:29). The stones carried names to be remembered, not energy to be absorbed.

The Law forbids the rest. There was not to be found in Israel "an enchanter, or a witch, Or a charmer" (Deuteronomy 18:10-11). A stone sold to heal by its energy is a charm by another name.

## The Verdict

The Om pendant wears the sacred sound of Brahman. The chakra stones sell a Hindu body map, painted in Western rainbow colors, as medicine. Neither has any place on a believer's neck or nightstand. Both belong under the oak.

Revelation shows where precious stones end up in Yahuah's plan: "the foundations of the wall of the city were garnished with all manner of precious stones" (Revelation 21:19) — set in His city, not hung on a cord to draw power.
