## The Reverse of the Church

Islam kept pieces of the Law with no Lamb. The church kept the Lamb's name and threw out the Law. Judaism stands at the opposite end from the church. It held on to the Law, then refused the Lamb the Law pointed to, and buried the Law itself under tradition.

## What Judaism Kept

Paul asked what advantage the Jew had and answered, "Much every way: chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God" (Romans 3:1-2). The Hebrew Scriptures came to the world through their hands.

Judaism kept the Shema: "Hear, O Israel: Yahuah our God is one Yahuah" (Deuteronomy 6:4). No triad and no three-in-one. A Jew who recites the Shema confesses the same words Yahushua called the first commandment (Mark 12:29).

Judaism kept a seventh-day Sabbath, the clean table of Leviticus 11, the appointed feasts of Leviticus 23, and circumcision. Through centuries of exile and slaughter, the Jewish people kept these things when the church called them bondage.

Paul gave them this witness: "I bear them record that they have a zeal of God, but not according to knowledge" (Romans 10:2).

## Where Judaism Went Wrong

**It refused the Messiah.** "He came unto his own, and his own received him not" (John 1:11). Yahushua told the leaders of His day, "had ye believed Moses, ye would have believed me: for he wrote of me" (John 5:46). Isaiah described the Servant "wounded for our transgressions" and "brought as a lamb to the slaughter" (Isaiah 53:5-7), and Daniel said Messiah would "be cut off, but not for himself" (Daniel 9:26). The Law and the prophets point to Him, and Judaism kept the Law while turning away from the One it points to. Paul said a veil still lies over their heart when Moses is read, and "when it shall turn to the Master, the veil shall be taken away" (2 Corinthians 3:14-16).

**It lost the blood.** "The life of the flesh is in the blood… it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul" (Leviticus 17:11). Since the Temple fell in 70 AD, Judaism has had no altar, no priest, and no blood. It answered by teaching that prayer, repentance, and charity now stand in place of the sacrifice. That is a Law without its own atonement. Yahuah had already provided the Lamb (Genesis 22:8), slain at Pesach about forty years before the Temple fell.

**It buried the commandments under tradition.** Yahushua told the scribes and Pharisees, "Full well ye reject the commandment of God, that ye may keep your own tradition" (Mark 7:9), and "ye made the commandment of God of none effect by your tradition" (Matthew 15:6). That tradition was later written down as the Talmud and became the real rule of Judaism. A Jew today keeps the Sabbath by thousands of fences men built around it, not by the plain words of Moses.

**It changed Yahuah's calendar.** Judaism now keeps a calendar calculated in advance, fixed under Hillel II around 359 AD, in place of the barley and the renewed moon. It reckons its days from sundown to sundown, a Babylonian practice brought back from exile. It ties the Sabbath to Saturday, the day of Saturn, in an unbroken Roman week, while Scripture counts it by the moon Yahuah appointed "for seasons" (Psalm 104:19).

**It stopped saying the Name.** Yahuah said, "this is my name for ever, and this is my memorial unto all generations" (Exodus 3:15). Judaism forbade speaking it and put a title in its place. The people Yahuah gave His Name to became the people who hid it.

## Who Is a Jew

Paul defined it: "he is not a Jew, which is one outwardly… But he is a Jew, which is one inwardly; and circumcision is that of the heart" (Romans 2:28-29). Abraham's promise was to his Seed, singular, "which is Messiah" (Galatians 3:16), "and if ye be Messiah's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise" (Galatians 3:29). Belonging to Israel runs through the Messiah, not through bloodline alone.

The door is not shut. Paul said the natural branches "if they abide not still in unbelief, shall be graffed in" again (Romans 11:23).

## The Lesson for the Church

The church looks at Judaism and sees a people bound by Law. Judaism looks at the church and sees a people with no Law. Both are right about the other. The Jew has the commandments without the testimony of the Messiah. The church has the testimony without the commandments. Each one holds half of what Yahuah gave.

## Conclusion

Judaism is the Law kept without the Lamb, and the Law smothered by men's tradition. Its people confessed the one God, guarded the Scriptures, and kept the Sabbath and the clean table through every persecution. Then they refused the One that all of it pointed to. The answer is not to throw out the Law as the church did, but to come to the Lamb and keep the Law as He did.

Revelation names the remnant the dragon makes war with, and they hold both halves: they "keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Yahushua Messiah" (Revelation 12:17).
