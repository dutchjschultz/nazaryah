News cycles will tell you who fired what missile at whom. They will not tell you who is in Jacob's land, by whose authority, or what role Scripture gives them in the closing record of the age.

The structure that signed the Messiah's death warrant in the first century — Edomite front men running the daily system, Rome holding the throne behind — never collapsed. It restructured. The Roman institution rebranded. The Edomite claim to Jacob's land rebranded. The financial network rebranded (Roth Schild — red shield — the shield of Edom). The front keeps changing. The rider in the saddle does not.

Court Appeal II, filed between Chapters 17 and 18 of *The Courtroom of Heaven*, places the full evidence before the heavenly courtroom — the Woman, the Beast in all its facets, and the Second Beast that has been providing the cover.
