## The Verse

Hosea sets two fields side by side. Israel is told to "sow to yourselves in righteousness" and "reap in mercy" (Hosea 10:12), and in the next breath is reminded that it had "plowed wickedness" and "reaped iniquity" (Hosea 10:13). Every seed returns as its own kind of harvest. The Hebrew for mercy is *chesed*, the covenant loyalty Yahuah shows to those who walk in His ways. Moses names who receives it: Yahuah shows mercy "unto thousands of them that love me, and keep my commandments" (Exodus 20:6; Deuteronomy 7:9). Yahushua says the same: "If ye keep my commandments, ye shall abide in my love" (John 15:10). That *chesed* is the harvest Hosea promises, gathered by the one who sowed righteousness: the keeper of the Law.

## The Order Inside the Verse

Read alone, the first line sounds like works first and mercy earned. The verse does not end there. Israel is told to break up the fallow ground and seek Yahuah "till he come and rain righteousness upon you." The righteousness rained down (*tsedeq*) comes from above before any righteousness (*tsedaqah*) is sown below. Yahuah sends the rain; His people sow what has fallen on them. The order is deliverance first, law-abiding second, and mercy as the harvest of a life spent keeping what was given.

## The Same Harvest in the New Testament

Paul works the same field. Yahuah "is not mocked," and whatever a man sows, he reaps: corruption from the flesh, life everlasting from the Spirit (Galatians 6:7-8). Sowing to the flesh is the carnal mind that is "not subject to the law" (Romans 8:7). Sowing to the Spirit is the walk of the delivered, with the Law written on the heart. The modern teaching that obedience after deliverance gains nothing and risks nothing ignores the farming rule both Testaments share: what is planted determines what is gathered.

## The Final Harvest

Revelation closes the season with a sickle thrust into a ripe earth (Revelation 14:15). The blessing at the end rests on those who "do his commandments," for theirs is the right to the tree of life (Revelation 22:14).
