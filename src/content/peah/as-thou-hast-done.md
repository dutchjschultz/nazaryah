"For the day of Yahuah is near upon all the heathen: as thou hast done, it shall be done unto thee: thy reward shall return upon thine own head" (Obadiah 1:15). The cup comes back the same way: "as ye have drunk upon my holy mountain, so shall all the heathen drink continually" (Obadiah 1:16). Edom would be paid in its own coin. The common teaching is that a believer's deeds will never come into the judgment, since all is covered. Obadiah states the rule Yahuah judges by, and the rule does not change.

David knew it: "thou renderest to every man according to his work" (Psalm 62:12).

Yahushua said the measure a man uses comes back to him: "with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again" (Matthew 7:2).

Paul wrote it to the assemblies: "Be not deceived; God is not mocked: for whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap" (Galatians 6:7). He told the Romans that Yahuah "will render to every man according to his deeds" (Romans 2:6).

The usual escape is that this is only for unbelievers. Paul included himself and the assembly: "we must all appear before the judgment seat" of Messiah, "that every one may receive the things done in his body ... whether it be good or bad" (2 Corinthians 5:10). Entry is by the blood alone. The walk after it is still weighed.

The conclusion is plain. Yahuah judges by deeds, for Edom and for His own. Deliverance through the blood is the door, and what a man sows on the other side of it, he reaps. Yahushua's last promise says the same: "my reward is with me, to give every man according as his work shall be" (Revelation 22:12).
