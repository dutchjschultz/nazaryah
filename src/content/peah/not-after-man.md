Paul says his gospel "is not after man. For I neither received it of man, neither was I taught it, but by the revelation of Yahushua Messiah" (Galatians 1:11-12). Many were taught that Paul brought a new gospel, different from Moses and from the Law. The revelation he received was not new. It matched what was already written.

Moses foretold the Prophet to come: "I will raise them up a Prophet ... and will put my words in his mouth" (Deuteronomy 18:18). Yahushua said where His own message came from: "had ye believed Moses, ye would have believed me: for he wrote of me" (John 5:46).

Paul told King Agrippa what his revealed gospel contained. He said "none other things than those which the prophets and Moses did say should come" (Acts 26:22). Before Felix he said he was "believing all things which are written in the law and in the prophets" (Acts 24:14).

The usual escape is that Paul's gospel replaced the old one. Galatians answers it: "the scripture ... preached before the gospel unto Abraham" (Galatians 3:8). The gospel Paul received was the one Abraham heard.

The conclusion is plain. Paul's gospel came by revelation, and it agreed with Moses and the prophets word for word. Revelation says what all true prophecy carries: "the testimony of Yahushua is the spirit of prophecy" (Revelation 19:10).
