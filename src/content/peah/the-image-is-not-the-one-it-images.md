Trinitarian teaching reads this verse as proof that the Son shares the very being of God. "Person" is taken to mean a person of the godhead. The Greek word is hypostasis (G5287), being or substance, the same word Hebrews uses in 3:14 ("confidence") and 11:1 ("substance"). It does not mean a person in a godhead.

Read the two key words. "Brightness" is apaugasma (G541), radiance, the light that shines out from a source. Yahuah is the light; the Son is the one who bears and shows it. "Express image" is charakter (G5481), the stamp pressed into wax or metal by a seal. The stamp carries the exact likeness of the seal, but it is not the seal. Man was made in the image of God (Genesis 1:27), and the Son is that image fulfilled (2 Corinthians 4:4; Colossians 1:15). The verse ends with the Son seated "on the right hand of the Majesty on high." One sits beside the other. Two are in view, not one.
