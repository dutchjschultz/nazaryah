When Philip asked to see the Father, Yahushua answered, "he that hath seen me hath seen the Father" (John 14:9). Many read that as "I am the Father," or "I am God."

The next verse tells what he meant: "the words that I speak unto you I speak not of myself: but the Father that dwelleth in me, he doeth the works" (John 14:10). The words were the Father's. The works were the Father's. A few verses later he says it again: "the word which ye hear is not mine, but the Father's which sent me" (John 14:24).

Philip saw the Father the way a man sees the sender in the one he sends. The Son was the one sent. The Father was the one dwelling in him and doing the works.
