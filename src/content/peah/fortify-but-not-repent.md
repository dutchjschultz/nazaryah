Yahuah mocks Nineveh's preparations: "Draw thee waters for the siege, fortify thy strong holds: go into clay, and tread the morter" (Nahum 3:14). It will not help: "There shall the fire devour thee" (Nahum 3:15). The city got ready in every way but one. It never turned to Yahuah, as it once had under Jonah.

Isaiah saw Jerusalem do the same. They looked "to the armour," and "gathered together the waters of the lower pool," but "ye have not looked unto the maker thereof" (Isaiah 22:8-9, 11).

Yahushua named the only defense that works: "except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish" (Luke 13:3).

Paul warned how the end comes on the unready: "when they shall say, Peace and safety; then sudden destruction cometh upon them" (1 Thessalonians 5:3).

Many are taught to prepare for the end with supplies, plans, and walls. Nahum shows a city that did all of that and fell anyway. Preparing the house without turning the heart is preparing to lose.

The conclusion is plain. Yahuah looks for repentance, not fortification. Revelation shows men under His judgments who still "repented not of their deeds" (Revelation 16:11).
