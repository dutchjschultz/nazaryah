## The Old Path

Yahuah charged the priests to "put difference between holy and unholy, and between unclean and clean" (Leviticus 10:10). In Leviticus 11 He named the beasts, fish, and birds Himself, so the line was never left to appetite or conscience. He gave the reason, "ye shall be holy; for I am holy," right after naming Himself "Yahuah that bringeth you up out of the land of Egypt" (Leviticus 11:44-45). Deliverance came first, and holiness followed. Noah already knew the clean beasts from the unclean long before Sinai (Genesis 7:2), and Ezekiel condemned the priests who "put no difference" (Ezekiel 22:26). Yahuah told Abram, "walk before me, and be thou perfect," *tamim*, whole (Genesis 17:1), and Peter carried the same command to the believers: "Be ye holy; for I am holy" (1 Peter 1:16). Holiness is not a feeling or a guess. It is the line Yahuah drew, walked by a people He has already delivered.

## What Islam Teaches

Islam divides food into *halal*, permitted, and *haram*, forbidden. The Quran forbids four things by name: animals found dead, blood, the flesh of swine, and anything slaughtered in the name of any but God (Surah 2:173; 5:3; 6:145; 16:115). It also forbids wine and intoxicants (Surah 5:90). Permitted animals must be slaughtered by cutting the throat with the name of God spoken over them, so the blood drains out.

Outside those rules much is allowed. The camel is eaten, and is even offered at the great yearly sacrifice of Eid al-Adha. The Quran says the game of the sea is lawful (Surah 5:96), and most Muslim schools of law allow shrimp, crab, and shellfish, though the Hanafi school limits seafood to true fish.

## What Islam Kept

Islam kept more of the food laws than the church. The Muslim will not touch pork, will not eat blood, will not eat what died of itself, and will not eat meat offered to idols. Set that beside the letter the apostles sent to the Gentile believers: abstain "from pollutions of idols, and from fornication, and from things strangled, and from blood" (Acts 15:20). The Muslim keeps nearly the whole of that list, while the church that claims the apostles eats it all. Islam also kept the truth that food is a matter of obedience to God, not of taste, and that a believer is set apart by what he refuses.

## Where Islam Falls

**It kept half the line Yahuah drew.** Leviticus 11 does not stop at swine. It names the camel first: "the camel, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you" (Leviticus 11:4). It forbids every creature in the waters that has not "fins and scales" (Leviticus 11:10), which takes in shrimp, crab, and every kind of shellfish. Islam drew its line from the Quran, not from Moses, and so it eats what Yahuah called "an abomination unto you."

**It puts holy food on the altar of another house.** The camel slaughtered at Eid al-Adha honors the story of Abraham's sacrifice, and most Muslims tell that story with Ishmael on the altar. Scripture names the son: "thine only son Isaac" (Genesis 22:2). An unclean beast offered to remember the wrong son is not a holy offering.

**It seeks holiness without the deliverance that comes first.** In Leviticus, Yahuah brought Israel out of Egypt, and then said "ye shall therefore be holy" (Leviticus 11:45). The order matters. Islam has the rules but no Passover Lamb, and it denies that Yahushua died. Clean food cannot make a man clean. Yahuah delivers through the blood of the Lamb He provided, and only then does the food law become the walk of a set-apart people, not a ladder to climb.

## The Verdict

Islam has the strictest table of any religion that has not kept Leviticus, and it puts the church to shame on pork and blood. But it kept only half of the line, eating the camel and the shellfish that Yahuah named unclean, and it walks its food laws with no Lamb at the door. Holiness that skips the deliverance is only diet.

Revelation describes the city of the set-apart: "there shall in no wise enter into it any thing that defileth" (Revelation 21:27). The ones who enter are those "written in the Lamb's book of life" (Revelation 21:27). A clean table without the Lamb's book does not open the gate.
