## The Old Path

On the fourth day Yahuah set lights in the sky "for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years" (Genesis 1:14). The seasons are *moadim*, appointed meetings, and Yahuah calls them "my feasts" (Leviticus 23:2). "He appointed the moon for seasons" (Psalm 104:19). The sun turns the year, and its first month is Abib (Deuteronomy 16:1; Exodus 12:2). The moon numbers the month from the new moon (Psalm 81:3). The Sabbaths fall on the eighth, fifteenth, twenty-second, and twenty-ninth of each month, and the day runs from dawn to dusk. The appointments still stand, kept "from one new moon to another, and from one sabbath to another" (Isaiah 66:23).

## What Islam Teaches

Islam keeps a calendar of twelve moon months and nothing else. It is called the Hijri calendar because it counts its years from Muhammad's flight from Mecca to Medina in AD 622, and it was set in place under the caliph Umar. A month begins when the thin crescent is seen after the new moon, though many Muslim countries now work it out by calculation. Each day of the calendar begins at dusk.

Twelve moon months come to about 354 days, eleven days short of the sun's year, and Islam never adds a month to catch up. Before Islam the Arabs did insert extra months, and the Quran forbids it. It says the number of months with God is twelve (Surah 9:36) and calls the postponing of months an increase in unbelief (Surah 9:37). So the whole calendar slides backward through the seasons. Ramadan, the month of fasting, walks the full circle of the seasons in about thirty-three years.

The weekly gathering is the Friday noon prayer (Surah 62:9). Friday is not a day of rest. The very next verse tells the faithful to go back out into the land once the prayer is done (Surah 62:10).

## What Islam Kept

Islam kept the moon. It counts its months from the renewed moon as Israel did, and the Quran says the new moons are set as times for the people and for the pilgrimage (Surah 2:189). That is close to the psalm: "He appointed the moon for seasons" (Psalm 104:19). The Quran also says God made the sun a shining light and the moon a light, and measured out its stages so men would know the count of years (Surah 10:5). Islam kept the sky as a clock set by God, not by man. That is more than the church kept.

## Where Islam Falls

**It threw away the sun.** Genesis names both lights for "days, and years" (Genesis 1:14), and the Quran itself names both (Surah 10:5). But the Hijri year is counted by the moon alone, so the sun does nothing in it. Yahuah tied His first month to the season: "Observe the month of Abib" (Deuteronomy 16:1). Abib means green ears of grain. A month that drifts into winter can never be Abib. The feast of ingathering was "in the end of the year, when thou hast gathered in thy labours out of the field" (Exodus 23:16). A calendar that slides through every season cannot keep a harvest feast at harvest.

**It forbade the very correction the sun requires.** Surah 9:37 was aimed at Arab chiefs who shifted months for war and trade, and that abuse was real. But the cure cut off the sun's witness altogether. When a twelve-month year falls short of the sun, Yahuah's calendar lets a month open before the sun reaches its mark and simply does not count it as Abib. Islam has no way to do that.

**It leans on the crescent.** Waiting for the crescent to be seen makes the month depend on weather, place, and eyesight. Torah never commands anyone to sight a crescent. Israel knew the new moon was coming the day before it arrived (1 Samuel 20:5, 18).

**It has a gathering but no rest.** Yahuah called the Sabbath "my holy day" (Isaiah 58:13) and a sign "between me and you throughout your generations" (Exodus 31:13). Islam gathers on Friday at noon and sends men back to work.

## The Verdict

Islam read half the sky. It kept the moon and lost the sun, kept a gathering and lost the rest, and kept twelve months that never stay in their seasons. Its calendar cannot place one feast of Leviticus 23 where Yahuah put it.

Revelation shows the tree of life bearing "twelve manner of fruits," yielding "her fruit every month" (Revelation 22:2). Fruit comes in its season. In the new earth the months still bring the harvest at the right time, for the Maker of both lights still keeps His calendar.
