## The Old Path

At Horeb, Israel "heard the voice of the words, but saw no similitude; only ye heard a voice" (Deuteronomy 4:12). Moses drew the command straight from that: take heed "lest ye corrupt yourselves, and make you a graven image, the similitude of any figure" (Deuteronomy 4:15-16). The second commandment forbids "any graven image, or any likeness," and forbids bowing down to them (Exodus 20:4-5). Even the brass serpent Yahuah Himself commanded (Numbers 21:8-9) became an idol, and Hezekiah broke it in pieces and called it "Nehushtan," a piece of brass (2 Kings 18:4). Isaiah asked, "To whom then will ye liken God?" (Isaiah 40:18), and Paul told Athens that the divine is not "like unto gold, or silver, or stone, graven by art and man's device" (Acts 17:29). Yahuah gave His people a reminder that is no image at all: fringes on the garment, so that they would "remember all the commandments of Yahuah, and do them" (Numbers 15:39).

## What Islam Teaches

Islam is known for refusing images in its places of prayer. A mosque has no statue, no painting of God, and no picture of Muhammad. The Quran tells of Abraham standing against his father's people and asking what these images were to which they were so devoted (Surah 21:52), and of Abraham breaking their idols (Surah 21:58). The ban on pictures of living things comes mostly from the hadith, the sayings of Muhammad.

Yet at the center of Islam stands the Kaaba in Mecca, the cube every Muslim faces five times a day in prayer. Set into its eastern corner is the Black Stone. Pilgrims circle the Kaaba seven times, and each one who can reach the stone kisses it or touches it; those who cannot reach it point to it as they pass. Muslim tradition holds that it came down from heaven and was set in place by Abraham and Ishmael. A well-known report has Umar, the second caliph, telling the stone that he knew it was only a stone that could neither harm nor help, and that he kissed it only because he had seen Muhammad do so. Muslims say plainly that they serve God and not the stone.

## What Islam Kept

Islam kept the plain reading of the second commandment better than most of the world. It makes no image of God, and it refuses to picture any prophet. A Muslim would agree with Isaiah that nothing can be set beside God to compare Him with (Isaiah 40:18).

## Where Islam Falls

**It kept a sacred object at the very center.** The commandment does not stop at images of God. It forbids bowing down to any made or chosen thing as holy (Exodus 20:5). The Kaaba held idols before Islam, and Muslim history itself records that Muhammad cleared them out when he took Mecca in 630 AD. The idols went, but the Black Stone, already honored by the Arabs of Mecca before Islam, stayed and is kissed to this day. Umar knew it had no power and kissed it anyway.

**Israel had a Nehushtan too.** The brass serpent was given by Yahuah, and it pointed to deliverance (Numbers 21:8-9). Seven hundred years later Israel was burning incense to it. Hezekiah did not keep it as a treasured relic of Moses. He broke it and called it what it was, a piece of brass (2 Kings 18:4). Whatever the origin of the Black Stone, a stone kissed by millions as part of a holy rite is the very thing Hezekiah broke.

**It turned the prayer toward a place.** Every Muslim prayer is aimed at a building of stone. Yahushua told the woman at the well that the hour had come when men would no longer serve the Father in one mountain or one city, but "in spirit and in truth" (John 4:21-23). A fixed stone shrine at the center of prayer is the old pattern of the nations, not the new.

## The Verdict

Islam broke the idols and kept the stone. It forbids every picture and then sends its people to kiss a rock set in the corner of a shrine. That is not the Old Path. The Old Path gave Israel no image and no sacred stone to kiss; it gave a fringe to remind them to do the commandments (Numbers 15:39).

Revelation shows the city where no shrine remains: "I saw no temple therein: for the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are the temple of it" (Revelation 21:22). No cube, no stone, no corner to face. In that day His servants "shall see his face" (Revelation 22:4), and no stone will be needed to stand in His place.
