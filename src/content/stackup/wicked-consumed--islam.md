## The Old Path

Fire went out from Yahuah and "devoured" Nadab and Abihu (Leviticus 10:2). Sodom was left "brimstone, and salt, and burning" (Deuteronomy 29:23), and Peter says Yahuah turned those cities "into ashes" (2 Peter 2:6). Malachi says the coming day "shall burn as an oven," leaving the wicked "neither root nor branch," and they "shall be ashes under the soles of your feet" (Malachi 4:1, 3). David says the wicked "shall consume; into smoke shall they consume away" (Psalm 37:20). Yahushua warned of Him who "is able to destroy both soul and body in hell" (Matthew 10:28), and Paul calls the end "everlasting destruction" (2 Thessalonians 1:9). The fire is eternal in what it does, not in how long it keeps burning. "The wages of sin is death" (Romans 6:23), not endless life in pain.

## What Islam Teaches

Islam calls the place of punishment Jahannam, the Fire. The Quran describes it as a fire "whose fuel is men and stones" (Surah 2:24; 66:6). It says the people of the Fire will abide in it forever (Surah 4:169; 72:23), and that whenever their skins are roasted through, they are given new skins so they may keep tasting the punishment (Surah 4:56). It says the guilty one in Jahannam "will neither die therein nor live" (Surah 20:74; 87:13).

The Quran even pictures the people of the Fire calling out to its keeper, Malik, asking that God put an end to them, and being told they will remain (Surah 43:77). Mainstream Islamic teaching adds that believing Muslims who sinned may be punished there for a time and then brought out, while unbelievers stay forever.

## What Islam Kept

Islam kept the fact that judgment is real and that the wicked do not escape it. It kept a coming Day when every person stands before God, and it kept fire as the instrument of that judgment, just as Scripture does. Malachi's oven and Peter's ashes are both fire. Islam does not pretend the wicked will be excused, and it does not soften sin into a mistake. On those points it stands closer to the Prophets than the modern pulpit that has stopped preaching judgment at all.

## Where Islam Falls

**It keeps the wicked alive to suffer.** Scripture says the fire "devoured" (Leviticus 10:2) and left "ashes" (Malachi 4:3). The Quran's new skins exist for one purpose: to stop the fire from finishing what fire does. That reverses the plain pattern of Sodom, which burned once and is not burning today. Jude says Sodom suffered "the vengeance of eternal fire" (Jude 7), and Peter explains what that means: the cities were turned "into ashes" as "an ensample unto those that after should live ungodly" (2 Peter 2:6). Eternal fire is fire whose result never gets undone.

**It turns death into life.** "The soul that sinneth, it shall die" (Ezekiel 18:4). Death is the sentence. The Quran's punished soul "neither dies nor lives," which means it never receives the sentence Yahuah set. A man kept alive forever in pain has been given eternal life in a fire, and Scripture gives eternal life only to the righteous (Romans 6:23).

**It has the wicked begging for what Scripture already promised.** In the Quran, the people of the Fire ask to be ended and are refused (Surah 43:77). In Scripture, the end of the wicked is the very thing Yahuah promises: "the wicked shall not be: yea, thou shalt diligently consider his place, and it shall not be" (Psalm 37:10). The Quran's picture is crueler than the Judge of the Bible, who says, "I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked" (Ezekiel 33:11).

**It offers a way out without blood.** The teaching that sinful Muslims may burn for a while and then come out makes the fire a payment. Scripture knows no such payment. "It is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul" (Leviticus 17:11), and "without shedding of blood is no remission" (Hebrews 9:22). Suffering does not wash sin away. Only the Lamb Yahuah provided does.

## The Verdict

Islam is right that the fire is coming and right that the wicked will not dodge it. It is wrong about what the fire does. Scripture's fire consumes, and the Quran's fire preserves. A fire that must keep renewing skins to keep working is a fire that never finishes, and Yahuah's fire always finishes.

Revelation shows the end of that fire in one line: "death and hell were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death" (Revelation 20:14). It is called death, not a second life of torment. After it, "there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying" (Revelation 21:4), and no corner of the new earth is left burning.
