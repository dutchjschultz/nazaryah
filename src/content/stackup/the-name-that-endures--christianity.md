## The Old Path

At the burning bush Yahuah gave Moses His Name and bound it to time: "this is my name for ever, and this is my memorial unto all generations" (Exodus 3:15). Moses later warned Israel to "fear this glorious and fearful name, Yahuah thy God" (Deuteronomy 28:58). The psalmist sang, "Thy name, O Yahuah, endureth for ever" (Psalm 135:13), and Malachi saw a book of remembrance written for those who "thought upon his name" (Malachi 3:16). A title tells what someone is; a name tells who He is. Yahuah gave a Name to be remembered and spoken, and nothing in Scripture ever withdrew it. Every religion's answer is measured against that standing command.

## What Christianity Teaches

Where Moses wrote the Name, the King James Bible and nearly every English Bible print "the LORD" in capital letters, nearly seven thousand times. In other places they print "GOD" in capitals. The King James left the form JEHOVAH in only a handful of verses (Exodus 6:3; Psalm 83:18; Isaiah 12:2; 26:4). Most modern versions removed even those. In 2008 the Vatican instructed Catholic churches not to use the Name in their services.

In the New Testament, the Greek word *kurios*, "lord," is used for the Father and for the Son alike, and English Bibles put "Lord" for both. From the pulpit the matter is settled with one line: "It doesn't matter what you call Him. He knows your heart."

## What Christianity Kept

The church kept the prayer Yahushua taught, and millions still say every week, "Hallowed be thy name" (Matthew 6:9). It kept *Hallelujah*, which means "praise ye Yah," the short form of the Name the psalmist used: "extol him… by his name Yah" (Psalm 68:4). The King James kept JEHOVAH in a few verses, enough to show the reader that a Name stood behind the title. And the church kept the Son's own name, which carries the Father's: Yahushua, "Yahuah saves." The Name is still hidden in the church's own words. It simply stopped hearing it.

## Where Christianity Falls

**It traded the Name for the title Baal carried.** Jeremiah condemned prophets who "think to cause my people to forget my name… as their fathers have forgotten my name for Baal" (Jeremiah 23:27). *Baal* means lord, master, or owner. Israel forgot the Name by calling Yahuah "lord," and the church repeated it in print seven thousand times. The Greek *kurios* was no cleaner. It was the title of Caesar, and "Caesar is lord" was the oath of the empire.

**It blurred the Father and the Son.** David wrote, "Yahuah said unto my *adon*, Sit thou at my right hand" (Psalm 110:1). *Adon* is the word Sarah used of her husband Abraham (Genesis 18:12), a word for a man and a master. Yahushua used this verse to silence the Pharisees (Matthew 22:44-45). English Bibles print it "The LORD said unto my Lord," two lords side by side, and the reader is left to guess. Out of that fog the church built two persons who are both "Lord God," and then a third. Restore the Name and the fog lifts: Yahuah speaks, and His anointed man sits at His right hand. Peter preached the same: "God hath made that same Yahushua… both Master and Messiah" (Acts 2:36). Yahuah does not make Himself anything. He made the Son.

**It said the Name does not matter.** Yahuah said His Name was "for ever" (Exodus 3:15). The pulpit says it no longer matters. Malachi's book of remembrance was written for those who "thought upon his name" (Malachi 3:16), not for those who said it made no difference. A heart that loves the Father does not shrug at what He said to call Him.

## The Verdict

Christianity hid the Name under a title Jeremiah had already condemned, and in doing so it lost the line between the Father and the Son. The Name is still in its prayers and its songs, in Hallelujah and in the name of Yahushua itself, but the church has taught its people that none of it matters. Yahuah said it matters for ever.

Revelation gives the answer in the Son's own words to the overcomer: "I will write upon him the name of my God… and I will write upon him my new name" (Revelation 3:12). The risen Son still calls the Father "my God," and the Name written on His people is the Father's Name, kept distinct from His own.
