## The Question He Asked

At Caesarea Philippi, Yahushua asked His disciples, "Whom do men say that I the Son of man am?" Peter answered, "Thou art the Messiah, the Son of the living God," and Yahushua said that answer came from "my Father which is in heaven" (Matthew 16:13-17). Peter's answer has two parts: He is the Messiah, and He is the Son of the living God. Every religion's answer is measured against those two parts.

## What Christianity Teaches

The church teaches that Jesus is God the Son, the second person of a Trinity of three co-equal, co-eternal persons in one God. It teaches that He existed as God from all eternity, took on a human nature at Bethlehem, and was "fully God and fully man." This was set down in councils, not in Scripture. The Council of Nicaea in 325 AD declared the Son to be "of one substance" with the Father. The Council of Constantinople in 381 AD added the Spirit as a third person. The Council of Chalcedon in 451 AD defined the two natures in one person.

The church also calls Jesus "the Savior" and "Lord God," and in its hymns and prayers it often speaks to Him as if He were the Father Himself.

## What Christianity Kept

Of the five religions, the church kept the most facts about Him. It confesses that He was born of a virgin, that He is the Messiah and the Son, that He died on the tree, that His blood atones, that He rose bodily the third day, and that He will return. Islam denies the death. Judaism denies the Messiah. Hinduism and Buddhism deny the Son. The church holds all of these.

It even kept Peter's exact words. The trouble is what it did with them.

## Where Christianity Falls

**It turned "the Son of the living God" into "God the Son."** Peter confessed a Son of God, not a God the Son. Yahushua Himself called His Father "the only true God" and named Himself apart from that as the one "whom thou hast sent" (John 17:3). He said, "my Father is greater than I" (John 14:28). After the resurrection He said, "I ascend unto my Father, and your Father; and to my God, and your God" (John 20:17). Paul said, "to us there is but one God, the Father" (1 Corinthians 8:6). A Son who has a God is not that God.

**It made Him eternal and unbegotten.** A son has a beginning. Scripture says, "Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee" (Psalm 2:7). The angel told Mary that the holy one born of her would be called the Son of God "therefore," because the power of the Highest overshadowed her (Luke 1:35). His Sonship began in the womb, not in eternity.

**It made Him equal in knowledge and power.** Yahushua said of the day of His return, "of that day and that hour knoweth no man… neither the Son, but the Father" (Mark 13:32). He said, "I can of mine own self do nothing" (John 5:30). Equal persons do not have one who knows what the other does not.

**It gave Him the Father's title of Savior.** Yahuah said, "I, even I, am Yahuah; and beside me there is no saviour" (Isaiah 43:11). Yahuah saves. The Son provided the way: "God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son" (John 3:16). The church took the Giver's title and handed it to the Gift.

**It joined the pagan pattern.** A god who comes down from heaven in human form is the Hindu avatar and the old pagan story of the gods. Scripture says, "God is not a man" (Numbers 23:19), and calls the Son "the man Messiah Yahushua" (1 Timothy 2:5). In making the Son into God, the church moved closer to the temples of India than to the confession of Peter.

## The Verdict

The church kept every fact and changed the One they describe. It has the virgin birth, the blood, the empty tomb, and the return, and it put them all onto a God the Son that no apostle ever named. Peter's confession came from the Father. The church's confession came from a council.

Revelation settles it in the Son's own mouth. Risen and glorified, He says of the one who overcomes, "Him that overcometh will I make a pillar in the temple of my God… and I will write upon him the name of my God, and the name of the city of my God" (Revelation 3:12). Four times in one verse, the glorified Son calls Yahuah "my God."
