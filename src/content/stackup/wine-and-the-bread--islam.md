## The Old Path

Melchizedek met Abram with "bread and wine" (Genesis 14:18). The Law never separated the two: every burnt offering carried a meal offering and a drink offering beside it (Numbers 15:4-5). The night before He died, Yahushua gave His disciples both, the bread, "my body which is given for you," and the cup, "the new testament in my blood" (Luke 22:19-20). The cup is the blood and the entry, for "it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul" (Leviticus 17:11). The bread is the body and the walk, living "by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God" (Matthew 4:4). Paul joined them: "justified by his blood… saved by his life" (Romans 5:9-10). The cup starts a man. The bread finishes him.

## What Islam Teaches

Islam has no blood atonement and no cross. The Quran says the Jews did not kill Isa or crucify him, but it was made to appear so to them (Surah 4:157). It teaches that no soul bears the burden of another (Surah 6:164; 17:15; 35:18), so no one can carry another man's sin. A Muslim is forgiven by turning to God in repentance, called *tawbah*, and by God's mercy. On the Day of Judgment his deeds are weighed in the balance.

Islam does keep one sacrifice. At the feast of Eid al-Adha, Muslims slaughter a sheep, goat, or camel to remember Abraham's offering of his son, whom the Quran says God ransomed "with a great sacrifice" (Surah 37:107). But the Quran says of those animals that neither their meat nor their blood reaches God; what reaches Him is the devotion of the giver (Surah 22:37). The sacrifice is an act of obedience, not a covering for sin.

Islam also forbids wine. The Quran calls strong drink a filthy work of Satan to be avoided (Surah 5:90).

## What Islam Kept

Islam kept a great deal of the bread. It is a religion of daily obedience: set prayers, fasting, alms, a clean diet, and a law for all of life. It forbids eating blood and pork (Surah 2:173), as the Torah does (Leviticus 17:12; 11:7). It kept the story of Abraham and the ransom. Scripture tells the same: "God will provide himself a lamb," and Abraham "offered him up for a burnt offering in the stead of his son" (Genesis 22:8, 13). The Quran's "great sacrifice" is a memory of the substitute Yahuah provided. Islam kept the walk and kept the shadow of the Lamb.

## Where Islam Falls

**It removed the cup.** Scripture says the blood atones (Leviticus 17:11), and "without shedding of blood is no remission" (Hebrews 9:22). The Quran says the blood of the sacrifice does not reach God at all (Surah 22:37). The blood was carried into the sanctuary and sprinkled before Yahuah on the Day of Atonement (Leviticus 16:14-15). Islam kept the knife and threw out the blood.

**It forbids the very thing Yahuah provided.** The Quran says no one bears another's burden. Isaiah says, "Yahuah hath laid on him the iniquity of us all" (Isaiah 53:6), and "he bare the sin of many" (Isaiah 53:12). The ransom Abraham saw on the mountain was a life given in place of a life.

**It walks the road without the door.** The bread is the walk, and Islam has a walk. But the walk comes after the entry, never instead of it. Yahushua said, "I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in and out" (John 10:9). Islam sends a man down the road of prayer and fasting and then weighs his deeds. A road with no door at the start is a road that never reaches the house.

**It denies the death that opened the door.** If the Messiah was never crucified (Surah 4:157), there was no body given and no blood shed. Paul delivered "first of all… how that Messiah died for our sins according to the scriptures" (1 Corinthians 15:3). Without the death there is neither cup nor bread.

## The Verdict

Islam is all bread and no cup. It kept the walk and refused the door, kept the ram and refused its blood, kept Abraham's mountain and refused the Lamb that was provided there. Obedience without the blood leaves a man on a scale, hoping.

Revelation answers with the song of the redeemed to the Lamb: "thou wast slain, and hast redeemed us to God by thy blood out of every kindred, and tongue, and people, and nation" (Revelation 5:9). Yahuah paid the price, and the price was His Son. The blood the Quran says never reaches God is the blood that brought every nation to Him.
