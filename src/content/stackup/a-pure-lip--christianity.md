## The Old Path

Yahuah promised, "then will I turn to the people a pure language, that they may all call upon the name of Yahuah, to serve him with one consent" (Zephaniah 3:9). The Hebrew is *safah berurah*, a pure lip. At Babel Yahuah confounded the lip so that men "may not understand one another's speech" (Genesis 11:7); the pure lip undoes Babel. When the Spirit came upon the seventy elders they prophesied, and Moses wished "that all Yahuah's people were prophets" (Numbers 11:29). Joel promised the same Spirit on sons, daughters, servants, and handmaids (Joel 2:28-29). At Shavuot that promise came, and every man asked, "how hear we every man in our own tongue, wherein we were born?" (Acts 2:8). Yahuah "is not the author of confusion" (1 Corinthians 14:33). The gift He promised is a language understood, used to call on His Name. Every religion's answer is measured against that promise.

## What Christianity Teaches

For more than a thousand years the Roman Church held its Mass in Latin across the western world, long after the common people had stopped speaking it. Only after the Second Vatican Council in the 1960s did Rome allow the Mass in the local language, and the Latin Mass is still kept by some Catholics today.

On the other side of the church, the tongues movement that began in Topeka in 1901 and spread from the Azusa Street meetings in Los Angeles in 1906 taught that "speaking in tongues" is the sign of being filled with the Holy Spirit. What is practiced in most of those churches today is not a known language but a stream of syllables no one present can understand. It is often called a "prayer language," spoken privately to God. Linguists who have recorded and studied it have reported that it does not have the makings of a human language.

## What Christianity Kept

The church kept the Scriptures in the common tongue, at least on the Protestant side. Men such as Tyndale gave their lives to put the Bible into the language of the plowman. That is the pattern of Ezra, whose Levites "caused them to understand the reading" (Nehemiah 8:8).

## Where Christianity Falls

**The Latin Mass rebuilt Babel.** Paul said he would rather "speak five words with my understanding… than ten thousand words in an unknown tongue" (1 Corinthians 14:19). For a thousand years Rome gave its people ten thousand words in an unknown tongue and called it worship. Yahuah confounded the lip at Babel as a judgment (Genesis 11:7). Rome kept that judgment in place on purpose.

**Modern tongues are noise, not language.** At Shavuot the crowd said, "we do hear them speak in our tongues the wonderful works of God" (Acts 2:11). Paul said, "except ye utter by the tongue words easy to be understood, how shall it be known what is spoken? for ye shall speak into the air" (1 Corinthians 14:9). Luke names the spirit behind the Greeks' sacred speech when he tells of a girl "possessed with a spirit of divination" (Acts 16:16), in Greek a spirit of *python*, the name tied to the oracle at Delphi. Yahuah "is not the author of confusion" (1 Corinthians 14:33). Sound with no sense is the pattern of the pagan oracle, not of Shavuot.

**A "prayer language" is not prayer.** Yahushua taught His disciples to pray in plain words to the Father, "Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name" (Matthew 6:9). The church points to "groanings which cannot be uttered" (Romans 8:26), but a groaning that cannot be uttered is by definition not a stream of spoken syllables. A prayer the one praying cannot understand is not him speaking to Yahuah at all.

**The lip does not call on the Name.** Zephaniah's pure lip exists so that men "call upon the name of Yahuah." Whether in Latin, in English, or in syllables, the church calls on "the Lord" and has taught its people the Name does not matter.

## The Verdict

Christianity gave the world Bibles in its own tongues and then filled its worship with words no one understands: Latin for a thousand years, and now noise called the Spirit. Neither is the pure lip. Yahuah promised understood speech calling on His Name with one consent, and the church gives its people confusion and a title.

Revelation sends one message to every tongue on earth: "the everlasting gospel… to every nation, and kindred, and tongue, and people, saying with a loud voice, Fear God, and give glory to him" (Revelation 14:6-7). A loud voice, in every tongue, saying words that can be understood. That is how the pure lip sounds.
