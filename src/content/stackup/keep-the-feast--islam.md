## The Old Path

Yahuah gave Israel a memorial in Egypt, and it was a feast: "this day shall be unto you for a memorial; and ye shall keep it a feast to Yahuah throughout your generations; ye shall keep it a feast by an ordinance for ever" (Exodus 12:14). In Scripture, to remember is to do. The fringes were given so that Israel would "remember all the commandments of Yahuah, and do them" (Numbers 15:39). The feasts belong to Him: "the feasts of Yahuah… even these are my feasts" (Leviticus 23:2). On the night before He died, at a preparation meal on the 13th of Aviv, Yahushua took bread and said, "this do in remembrance of me" (Luke 22:19). He was not starting a church service. He was laying the Pesach memorial on Himself, the Lamb who died on the 14th at the hour the lambs were slain. Paul drew the line plainly: "Messiah our passover is sacrificed for us: Therefore let us keep the feast" (1 Corinthians 5:7-8). The memorial is the feast, kept as Yahuah gave it.

## What Islam Teaches

Islam keeps two great feasts and one holy month. Ramadan is the ninth month of the Islamic year, the month Muslims believe the Quran was first sent down (Surah 2:185). Through the whole month, Muslims fast from dawn to dusk, taking no food or drink in the daylight hours. The month ends with Eid al-Fitr, the feast of breaking the fast. The second feast, Eid al-Adha, falls during the days of the pilgrimage to Mecca. It remembers Abraham's willingness to offer up his son, whom most Muslims hold to be Ishmael, and the Quran says God "ransomed him with a great sacrifice" (Surah 37:107). Families who can afford it slaughter an animal and share the meat with the poor.

The Islamic calendar is purely lunar. Each month begins with the sighting of the new crescent, and the Quran forbids adding a month to keep the year in step with the seasons (Surah 9:36-37). So Ramadan moves about eleven days earlier every year and passes through every season of the sun.

## What Islam Kept

Islam kept more of the pattern than it knows. It numbers its months by the moon, as Israel did. It keeps a feast that remembers Abraham and a son spared by a ransom, which is the same story Genesis tells. Abraham told his son, "God will provide himself a lamb" (Genesis 22:8), and a ram caught in a thicket died in the boy's place (Genesis 22:13). Islam even kills an animal every year to remember it. It kept the shadow of a substitute.

## Where Islam Falls

**It kept the ram and refused the Lamb.** The ram in the thicket was not the end of Abraham's story. Abraham said Yahuah would provide "a lamb," and the Lamb came. John pointed to Him: "Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world" (John 1:29). Islam remembers the ransom every year and denies the very Lamb it pointed to. The Quran says the Messiah was never crucified (Surah 4:157). A feast of ransom without the Lamb is a memorial with nothing left to remember.

**Its feasts are not His feasts.** Yahuah named His appointed times Himself: Pesach, Hag HaMatzot, Bikkurim, Shavuot, Yom Teruah, Yom Kippur, and Sukkot (Leviticus 23). Ramadan is not among them, and neither is Eid. The Quran says Ramadan was chosen because of the Quran, not because Yahuah appointed it. Yahuah called His feasts "my feasts" (Leviticus 23:2), and no man may name another.

**Its year has no season.** Moses commanded, "Observe the month of Abib, and keep the passover" (Deuteronomy 16:1). Aviv is the month of the green ear, fixed to the spring, because the lights were given "for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years" (Genesis 1:14). The sun turns the year and the moon numbers the month. Islam kept the moon and threw away the sun, so its holy month wanders through summer and winter alike. Its crescent sighting also differs from place to place, so one feast falls on different days in different lands.

## The Verdict

Islam keeps feasts Yahuah never gave and has no Pesach at all. It remembers Abraham's ransom and refuses the Lamb that ransom pointed to, and its calendar has lost the season the sun was given to keep.

Revelation shows heaven keeping the memorial Islam denies: "Worthy is the Lamb that was slain to receive power, and riches, and wisdom, and strength, and honour, and glory, and blessing" (Revelation 5:12). The ram was a picture. The Lamb was slain, and every creature in heaven remembers it.
