## The Old Path

Yahuah promised Abraham "all the land of Canaan, for an everlasting possession" (Genesis 17:8). The Psalmist wrote, "The heaven, even the heavens, are Yahuah's: but the earth hath he given to the children of men" (Psalm 115:16), and "The righteous shall inherit the land, and dwell therein for ever" (Psalm 37:29). Yahushua said the same: "the meek… shall inherit the earth" (Matthew 5:5). The dead do not go anywhere at death. They "sleep in the dust of the earth" until they awake (Daniel 12:2), and Job knew his Redeemer would "stand at the latter day upon the earth" (Job 19:25). The hope is resurrection to "new heavens and a new earth, wherein dwelleth righteousness" (2 Peter 3:13). The inheritance was never heaven. It is the earth made new.

## What Islam Teaches

Islam teaches that on the Last Day the dead are raised in their bodies, judged, and sent either to the Fire or to Jannah, the Garden. The Quran describes the Garden again and again as gardens "beneath which rivers flow" (Surah 2:25). It speaks of rivers of water, milk, wine, and honey (Surah 47:15), of couches, fruit, and fine garments, and of companions with large, beautiful eyes for the men who enter (Surah 44:54; 56:22). Islamic teaching describes the Garden in levels, with the highest reserved for the most faithful, and the Quran says that on that Day the earth will be changed into another earth (Surah 14:48). Entry is decided when a person's deeds are weighed, and Islam teaches that even then a Muslim enters by the mercy of God.

## What Islam Kept

Islam kept a great deal of the shape of the truth. It holds to a bodily resurrection, not a soul floating away forever. It holds that judgment comes on one Day at the end, not at the moment of death. It pictures the reward as a garden with rivers and fruit, which is the language of Eden and of the tree of life.

The Quran even repeats the Psalm. It says that it was written in the Zabur, the Psalms, that the earth would be inherited by God's righteous servants (Surah 21:105). That is Psalm 37:29 carried into the Quran nearly word for word in meaning. On this point the Muslim has read his Psalms better than the church that sends its dead to heaven.

## Where Islam Falls

**It turns the inheritance into appetite.** The Garden of the Quran is a reward of food, wine, couches, and maidens. Yahushua answered men who pictured the resurrection as marriage and the pleasures of this life: "in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage" (Matthew 22:30). The inheritance is not the flesh rewarded. It is life restored in the presence of Yahuah. The word "paradise" itself comes from the old Persian word for a walled pleasure garden of kings, and Islam kept the Persian garden rather than the promised land.

**It leaves out the One the inheritance is about.** The center of the promise was never the rivers. It was Yahuah dwelling with His people: "I will be their God, and they shall be my people" (Jeremiah 31:33). A garden full of pleasures with its Maker kept far off is not the inheritance of Abraham.

**It comes in by the scales, not the blood.** Islam weighs deeds and hopes for mercy, but it has no Lamb. Scripture says the inheritance comes to those who are Messiah's: "if ye be Messiah's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise" (Galatians 3:29). The seed is one, Messiah (Galatians 3:16), and Islam denies that He died. Without His blood no one enters, and after entry the delivered walk in Yahuah's commandments, not to earn the Garden, but because they are already heirs.

## The Verdict

Islam kept the resurrection, the one Day of judgment, the garden picture, and even the Psalm that says the righteous inherit the earth. It lost the heart of the promise. It turned the inheritance into a Persian pleasure garden, earned on the scales, with no Lamb at the door and no Father dwelling among His people.

Revelation shows the true Garden. John saw "a pure river of water of life… proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb," and on either side "the tree of life" (Revelation 22:1-2). And it is not far away: "his servants shall serve him: and they shall see his face" (Revelation 22:3-4). The river comes from the throne of the Lamb whom Islam denies.
