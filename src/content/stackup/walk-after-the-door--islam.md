## The Old Path

Abram "believed in Yahuah; and he counted it to him for righteousness" (Genesis 15:6), and afterward Yahuah said Abraham "kept my charge, my commandments, my statutes, and my laws" (Genesis 26:5). Israel was spared in Egypt by the blood on the door, "when I see the blood, I will pass over you" (Exodus 12:13), and only then was led to Sinai. Moses said the commandment "is very nigh unto thee, in thy mouth, and in thy heart, that thou mayest do it" (Deuteronomy 30:14). The psalmist said, "I will walk at liberty: for I seek thy precepts" (Psalm 119:45), and Solomon called the commandment "a lamp" and the law "light" (Proverbs 6:23). Deliverance comes through the blood Yahuah provided; Torah is what walking looks like on the other side of it. Torah was never the door, and the door was never the end of the road. Every religion's answer is measured against that order.

## What Islam Teaches

Islam is a religion of the walk. Its law is called *sharia*, a word whose root meaning is a path to water. The Muslim life is built on five pillars: the confession of faith, prayer five times a day, giving to the poor, fasting in the month of Ramadan, and the pilgrimage to Mecca once in a lifetime for those able to make it.

How a Muslim stands with God at the end is decided by his deeds. The Quran says that on the Day of Judgment the deeds will be weighed: "as for one whose scales are heavy, he will be in a pleasant life. But as for one whose scales are light, his refuge will be an abyss" (Surah 101:6-9). No one can carry another's sin, for "no bearer of burdens will bear the burden of another" (Surah 6:164). Animals are still sacrificed at the feast of Eid al-Adha, but the Quran says of them, "their meat will not reach Allah, nor will their blood, but what reaches Him is piety from you" (Surah 22:37).

## What Islam Kept

Islam kept the walk. It never taught that a man may believe and then live as he pleases. It kept set times of prayer, as Daniel prayed three times a day (Daniel 6:10). It kept fasting, giving to the poor, and the washing of the body. It kept pork off the table (Leviticus 11:7). On that point the Muslim stands closer to Abraham, who "kept my charge" (Genesis 26:5), than the church does.

## Where Islam Falls

**It has no door.** Israel did not walk out of Egypt and earn the Passover by walking well. The blood was put on the door first, and the walk came after (Exodus 12:13). Islam has the walk and no door. With nothing to enter by, the walk itself becomes the way in, and a man's whole hope rests on how his scales tip at the end.

**It refuses the blood.** The Quran says blood does not reach God (Surah 22:37). Torah says the opposite: "it is the blood that maketh an atonement for the soul" (Leviticus 17:11), and "without shedding of blood is no remission" (Hebrews 9:22). Islam is right that one man cannot simply carry another's guilt. That is why Yahuah did not lay the burden on any man of His choosing; He provided the Lamb Himself (Genesis 22:8), and Yahushua was offered as "the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world" (John 1:29). Without that blood, there is no door to walk through.

**It walks a path Yahuah did not give.** The path Islam walks is not Torah. Its prayers face Mecca, its fast follows the month of Ramadan, and its feasts are not the feasts of Yahuah in Leviticus 23. Moses said the commandment was already "very nigh unto thee" (Deuteronomy 30:14). Islam came centuries later with a different path and called it the straight one.

## The Verdict

Islam shames the church by walking when the church will not, but it walks with no door behind it. It has prayer, fasting, alms, and clean food, and it has no blood, no Lamb, and no assurance, only scales. That is the error of mixing the door and the walk: the walk is made to carry a weight it was never given.

Revelation sets the two in their right order. The great multitude "washed their robes, and made them white in the blood of the Lamb" (Revelation 7:14), and only then: "Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life" (Revelation 22:14). First the blood, then the walk.
