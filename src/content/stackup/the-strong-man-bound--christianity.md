## The Old Path

In the garden Yahuah promised that the seed of the woman would bruise the serpent's head (Genesis 3:15). Yahushua cast out devils "by the Spirit of God" and said no man can spoil a strong man's goods "except he first bind the strong man" (Matthew 12:28-29). He saw Satan "as lightning fall from heaven" (Luke 10:18) and said "now shall the prince of this world be cast out" (John 12:31). Through death He destroyed "him that had the power of death" (Hebrews 2:14). John saw the dragon bound "that he should deceive the nations no more, till the thousand years should be fulfilled" (Revelation 20:3). A thousand in Scripture is fullness: Yahuah keeps covenant "to a thousand generations" (Deuteronomy 7:9), and "one day is with the Lord as a thousand years" (2 Peter 3:8). The binding began in Yahushua's ministry and was finished at the cross. The thousand years is the present age, when the nations are turned "from the power of Satan unto God" (Acts 26:18).

## What Christianity Teaches

Most evangelical churches in America teach that the thousand years has not started. In the popular form, Jesus returns, Satan is chained, and Jesus reigns from Jerusalem over an earthly kingdom for a literal thousand years. Many add a rebuilt Temple, restored animal sacrifices, and a world still full of mortal people who are born, grow old, and die under that reign. At the end of it Satan is let loose, a final rebellion breaks out, and only then comes the last judgment. This teaching is called premillennialism, and its dispensational form, spread by study Bibles and prophecy preachers since the 1800s, is the one most churchgoers know. Not every church holds it. The Roman church and many older Protestant churches teach that the thousand years is the present age, but the future earthly kingdom is the teaching most American pews have heard.

The idea is old. Some early church writers held to a coming thousand-year kingdom, called chiliasm from the Greek *chilioi*, a thousand. The Greeks already had a thousand-year scheme of their own: in Plato's *Republic*, the souls of the dead make a journey of a thousand years before they return to new bodies. A counted thousand-year age fits that Greek journey better than it fits Scripture, where a thousand is fullness.

## What Christianity Kept

The church kept the chapter. It reads Revelation 20 and takes it seriously as Scripture. It holds that Satan is a real enemy and that he must be bound. It holds that Yahushua will return bodily and that judgment is coming. These are true, and many who sit under millennial preaching love the Word and want to understand it.

## Where Christianity Falls

**It moves the binding to the future when Yahushua said it was happening.** He did not say He would someday bind the strong man. He said He was casting out devils and plundering the house before their eyes (Matthew 12:28-29). He said "now" the prince of this world is cast out (John 12:31). He worked "by the Spirit of God," which Luke calls "the finger of God" (Luke 11:20), the Father's own power working through His Son, not a third person. The church that pushes the binding into the future denies what the Son said He did in the present.

**It turns the end into a new beginning.** Paul says that when Messiah comes, "then cometh the end," when the kingdom is delivered up to the Father and "the last enemy that shall be destroyed is death" (1 Corinthians 15:24-26). Yahushua said "the hour is coming" when all in the graves come forth, the good and the evil, in one hour (John 5:28-29). Peter says that in that day "the heavens shall pass away" and the earth's works "shall be burned up" (2 Peter 3:10). There is no room for a thousand more years of births and deaths after the return. The return ends death.

**It puts the shadow back.** A rebuilt Temple with animal sacrifices under the reign of Messiah would set aside His own blood. He entered the sanctuary "once" by "his own blood" (Hebrews 9:12), and "there is no more offering for sin" (Hebrews 10:18).
## The Verdict

Christianity kept the chapter and lost its time. It looks for a binding that already happened and a golden age that Scripture never promised. The strong man was bound at the cross, and the spoiling of his house is the gospel going to the nations now. When Yahushua returns, it is not to start an earthly age. It is to end death and hold the judgment.

Revelation says the dragon was bound "that he should deceive the nations no more, till the thousand years should be fulfilled" (Revelation 20:3). Every nation that has heard the Name since the cross is proof that the chain is already on.
